Astronomy 101

Embark on a cosmic journey exploring the wonders of our universe, from celestial bodies to space exploration.

How is the course structured?

Astronomy 101 is built around three core modules, each designed to give you a distinct and powerful lens through which to explore the universe.

🌌

Module 1

Theoretical Astronomy

Explore the fundamentals of astronomy and astrophysics through curated resources, lectures, and guided learning.

Celestial Mechanics Light & Spectroscopy Planetary Systems Exoplanets Stellar Evolution Black Holes Galaxies & Cosmology Dark Matter Observational Concepts
💻

Module 2

Computational Astronomy

Learn how astronomy and data science work together using Python and machine learning to analyze real cosmic data.

Python from Scratch NumPy & Pandas Data Visualization Linear & Logistic Regression Gradient Descent KNN · SVM · Naive Bayes Decision Trees Neural Networks & LLMs
🔭

Module 3

Observational Astronomy & Astrophotography

Gain practical experience in observing and imaging the night sky — from reading star maps to processing deep-space images.

Reading the Night Sky Star Hopping Telescope Handling Polar Alignment Astrophotography FITS Data & Archives Image Calibration & Stacking
Observation • Stellar Evolution • Cosmology • Gravitational Waves • Redshift • Planetary Science • Spectroscopy • Astrophysics • Radio Astronomy
